# Contributing to rok.vim

The Vim and Neovim plugin for rok: filetype detection, syntax, indentation, and
LSP wiring to `rokls`.

## Development

Point your plugin manager at a local checkout, or add this directory to your
`runtimepath`. The LSP needs `rokls` on your `PATH` (install the rok toolchain).

## Submitting

Open a pull request against `main`. By contributing you agree that your work is
licensed under both MIT and Apache-2.0, the project's dual license.
